Mearsheimer on the second-tier power that competes at the chokepoint the hegemon cannot police.

The offensive-realist reading of India’s magnet scheme is not that Delhi is closing a gap with Washington or Beijing at the register the great-power discipline usually surveys: fleets, doctrines, alliance systems. The American reshoring effort, from MP Materials at Mountain Pass to USA Rare Earth at Round Top, is real but under-scaled through 2028; the Chinese ninety-two-per-cent share is priced against precisely that timeline gap. Mearsheimer’s discipline names the specifically-material second-tier move as one that neither aligns with nor balances against the hegemon; it competes at the layer both great powers depend on and cannot yet displace. Munger on inversion, and what the moat actually is.

Charlie Munger’s working discipline was to invert the visible constraint until the actual constraint appeared. The visible constraint on India’s magnet ambition is the ore: monazite reserves, refining capacity, rare-earth-oxide throughput. IREL sits on the monazite at Chatrapur, refines at Aluva, and Sitharaman’s February corridors identify Odisha, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu as the geological substrate. Read forward, this is the story that reads. Read inverted, it is the wrong story. The actual constraint is the sintering line, and the actual moat is the process knowledge China has spent forty years compounding at Baotou, Ganzhou and Xiamen. The Ministry of Heavy Industries’ tender structure demonstrates that the operator has already inverted: the three lowest financial bidders receive assured NdPr oxide supply, which is the ore-side subsidy that would matter if the ore were the constraint; the two bidders who have actually built sintered magnets (Proterial, NEO Performance Materials) will win regardless of price because the scheme cannot afford to hand five slots to counterparties with no process. The specifically-material Munger inversion is that the eighteen non-process bidders are competing not for the manufacturing slot but for the technology-transfer partnership behind it: which Japanese group, which Korean firm, which American startup will supply the sintering process against which state land, capital and offtake are being underwritten. The winner of the scheme is not the Indian firm; it is the process holder who signs the specifically-material transfer agreement, and the tell is Proterial’s and NEO’s pricing rather than L&T’s.

Dalio on industrial-substitute supply as the middle-phase Big Cycle move.

Ray Dalio’s working thesis on the Big Cycle is that the middle phase of a great-power transition — when the incumbent order is no longer trusted but the challenger’s alternative is not yet trusted either — produces a specifically-material class of second-tier moves: industrial-substitute supply for chokepoints both great powers depend on. The Delhi Magnet sits inside that category. It is not aligned with the American reshoring push (MP Materials, USA Rare Earth, the Section-232 rare-earth investigation) and it is not aligned against Chinese state supply. It is a specifically-material third industrial output that Washington, Tokyo, Berlin and Seoul can each price separately as risk-diversification without owing anything to any of the others. The specifically-material Dalio tell is not whether Delhi wins market share this year; it is whether the offtake contracts signed inside 2027 route through Quad frameworks or through bilateral state-owned-buyer arrangements. The former reads as the middle phase of the cycle producing coordinated multipolar supply; the latter reads as it producing bilateral hedges that will not survive a Chinese counter-designation. The scheme’s architecture — state-underwritten, corridor-based, IREL-anchored, Quad-adjacent — is Delhi betting on the former.

Reading Note

The lenses used.

What’s driving this. Two layers carry the work. Structural: the specific mechanism is that neodymium sintered magnets are the narrowest chokepoint in the electric-vehicle, wind-turbine and precision-guided-munition supply chains, Chinese firms hold roughly ninety-two per cent of world sintering capacity, and neither the American reshoring push nor the Japanese process incumbency can close that gap on the timeline the buyer industries actually need. The Delhi scheme is a specifically-material industrial substitute at the layer where both great powers depend on inputs neither can substitute for. Coordination: the specific mechanism is that the Quad Critical Minerals Framework announced 26 May at the New Delhi Foreign Ministers’ meeting supplies the multilateral cover under which Japanese and Korean process holders can transfer sintering technology to India without triggering the specifically-material Chinese counter-designation that would follow a bare bilateral transfer; the eighteen non-process Indian bidders are effectively competing to be the local operator behind a foreign process partnership, and the awards will read primarily as technology-transfer partnerships dressed as capacity awards.

What’s not driving this. Not the ore. India has monazite reserves at Chatrapur since 1986 and refines high-purity NdPr oxide at Aluva; the Ministry could underwrite ore supply at any point in the past twenty years and did not. What changed is the process-transfer risk pricing after Beijing’s April 2025 dysprosium license tightening and the August 2026 sintering-line-adjacent additions to the MOFCOM list: the Japanese and Korean process holders now discount the option to sell their sintering knowledge to India at a state-underwritten Indian counterparty against the option to remain solo in a Chinese-controlled market. The Delhi scheme is the specifically-material discount rate India is offering to close that gap.
